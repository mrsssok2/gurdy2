# Phase 3 — mapping and navigation: design (2026-11-15)

Everything above the gait/policy node speaks `/cmd_vel`; Nav2 only adds one more publisher
(`collision_monitor` → `/cmd_vel`).

```
Gazebo ──/scan (gpu_lidar 360 rays, 10 Hz, 0.15–8 m, on top of the body)──┐
       ──/odom/ground_truth──> odom_tf_node ──> /odom + TF odom→base_link  │
                                                                           v
   slam mode:         slam_toolbox (online async)  → /map + TF map→odom ──> Nav2 (planner NavFn, MPPI Omni,
   localization mode: map_server + AMCL (Omni)     → /map + TF map→odom      costmaps, velocity_smoother,
                                                                           collision_monitor) → /cmd_vel
```

## Choices and why
- **Lidar** (`robot.yaml: sensors.lidar`, `lidar:=true`, only in the nav profile; RL launches unchanged).
  `depthimage_to_laserscan` from the RGB-D camera is the alternative (`scan_source:=depth`).
- **Odometry**: `odom_source:=ground_truth` (default; `odom_tf_node`, copies sim stamps → no `use_sim_time` CPU
  cost) or `odom_source:=ekf`: `leg_odometry_node` (stance-foot motion from `/joint_states` → `/odom/legs` twist
  with covariance; `/imu` → `/imu/ekf` with covariances filled in) → robot_localization `ekf_node`
  (`config/ekf.yaml`: leg vx, vy, wz + IMU yaw/yaw rate, 2D mode, `use_sim_time`) → `/odometry/filtered` and TF
  `odom→base_link`; Nav2 is pointed at `/odometry/filtered`. Math in `gurdy_core/leg_odometry.py`, drift test
  and numbers in `docs/results/phase3_leg_odometry.md` (offline; the Gazebo comparison is test-plan step 6).
  Ground-truth SLAM quality is the upper bound the EKF run is compared with.
- **slam_toolbox** `config/slam_toolbox.yaml`: min travel 0.05 m / 0.10 rad, scan matching on,
  max range 6 m, 0.05 m cells, loop closure on.
- **Nav2**: MPPI with the Omni motion model rather than DWB — shipped default, handles vy natively,
  rolled-out trajectories respect the acceleration limits; cost is CPU (batch 1000, 40 steps, 10 Hz) —
  if RTF drops, lower `batch_size` or switch to DWB. Limits vx 0.15 / vy 0.10 / wz 0.60 and the footprint
  are patched from `robot.yaml` at launch (`gurdy_navigation.footprint.patch_nav2_params`; a test keeps
  the committed YAML in sync). Footprint = convex hull of the six nominal-stance feet grown by foot radius
  + 4 cm: hexagon, circumradius 0.34 m. **Dead zone**: `velocity_smoother.deadband_velocity` (minimum speed) 0.05 m/s /
  0.15 rad/s — anything smaller is sent as zero, above the policy's own deadband (0.015 / 0.05) and its
  measured dead zone (±0.03 / ±0.10 before the 2.8 fix).
- **World** `flat_lab` (generated by `scripts/gen_world_flat_lab.py`): 8.5 × 6 m, two rooms, 1.0 m doorway,
  five coloured objects (also the Phase 4 detection targets), static primitives only.

## Known risks (to measure tomorrow)
- **Lidar tilt**: one horizontal layer 0.16 m above ground; walking pitch/roll of ±2–3° makes far rays hit
  the floor (at 0.16 m height a 1.5° pitch hits the floor at ~6 m). Mitigations if the map shows floor
  returns: lower `max_laser_range`/`obstacle_max_range`, widen `min_obstacle_height`, or mount higher.
- The body surges/sways every stride (tripod surge 0.013–0.137 m/s): scan matching sees smeared scans at
  0.1 s granularity; slam_toolbox's `minimum_time_interval 0.3` and travel thresholds mitigate it.
- MPPI CPU on top of Gazebo (RTF was 0.5–0.8 without it).
- gz lidar noise 1 cm; range clipping returns `inf` (handled by Nav2/slam_toolbox).
